PATIENT JOURNEY

How Sanjeevani Triage Works

From symptom onset to verified hospital admission in three streamlined steps.

01

Assess Symptoms

Use Gemini 3.8 Live Voice, AI Chatbot, or scan your prescription. The intelligent clinical engine evaluates severity according to Emergency Severity Index (ESI) standards.

02

Instant Allocation

For emergencies, the app initiates SOS dispatch with GPS tracking and ambient audio. For non-emergencies, it books a verified OPD token at the least congested nearby facility.

03

Zero-Wait Consultation

Present your verified QR/OPD token upon arrival at the triage desk. Your clinical history, vitals, and medication scans are already pre-loaded for the physician.

INSTALLATION GUIDE

How to Install the APK on Android

Follow these quick steps to sideload the native patient application safely.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download Android APK button above to save sanjeevani-triage-v1.0.apk to your device's Downloads folder.

2

Allow Installation

When prompted by Chrome or your browser, tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source".

3

Install & Open

Tap Install on the Android system installer dialog, then tap Open once installation finishes.

4

Grant Health Permissions

When prompted on first launch, allow Location (for hospital proximity), Microphone (for voice triage), and Google Health Connect permissions.
